Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Titan Estates and how we work

About Titan Estates

What is Titan Estates?

Titan Estates is a real estate and property investment consultancy, established in 2025. We work across Bangalore and Mysore, connecting genuine landowners, reputed developers and serious investors.

Where do you operate?

We currently serve Bangalore and Mysore.

Who do you work with?

We work with three groups: landowners looking for a trustworthy developer, developers looking for suitable land, and investors looking for well-researched property opportunities.

Our Services

What services do you offer?

We offer property investment consultancy, land acquisition, developer–landowner facilitation, investment opportunities, channel partner services, and deal coordination. You can read more on our About Us page.

I own land. How can Titan Estates help me?

We connect landowners with reputed developers who may be interested in acquiring or developing the land. Send us your details through the Contact page, including the location, size and any documents you have, and our team will get in touch.

I am a developer looking for land. Can you help?

Yes. We source and facilitate land opportunities for developers, and we help bring the right landowners to the table. Share your project requirements, preferred locations and budget through the Contact page.

What is a channel partner?

A channel partner works with us to introduce suitable projects and investment opportunities to clients. We work closely with reputed developers and property owners. If you are interested in partnering with us, please contact our team.

Properties and Investments

Do you list properties on the website?

Yes. You can browse available properties on our Properties page and filter by location, type, BHK, price and possession status. Listings are updated as new opportunities become available.

How do I know a property opportunity is genuine?

We focus on working with genuine property owners and established developers. We still recommend that every buyer or investor carry out their own due diligence, including checking title documents, approvals and the developer's track record, before making any decision.

Do you provide investment advice?

We help investors identify opportunities that match their goals and provide information to support their decisions. Investment decisions are your own, and we recommend taking professional legal and financial advice where needed.

Contact and Enquiries

How can I contact you?

You can call or WhatsApp us on 9901069834, email titanestates@gmail.com, or send an enquiry through our Contact page.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Please include your name, phone number, and a short description of what you are looking for, such as the location, property type, budget, or land size. The more detail you share, the faster we can respond with relevant options.

How long will it take to get a response?

Our team will get back to you as soon as possible after receiving your enquiry. For urgent matters, please call or WhatsApp us directly.

Still have a question?

Our team is happy to help. Reach out and we will get back to you.
